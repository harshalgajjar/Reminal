# 3.19.0

- Windows you mirror on a phone or tablet have a second way to be driven: a direct touch switch on the window's bar, next to the keyboard one. On, a tap clicks what is under your finger, one finger scrolls the content, a long press grabs and drags, two fingers pinch to zoom, and a two-finger tap right-clicks. Off, the window works as a trackpad, as before: one finger moves the cursor, a tap clicks there, two fingers scroll. Your choice is remembered in the browser for every window.
- Scrolling a window by finger keeps going after a flick and slows down the way a list on your phone does. Touching the screen stops it. A long page no longer takes a march of drags.
- A window shown smaller than its real size now scrolls by the same amount per swipe or wheel notch as it does at full size.

Fixed
- A fast two-finger swipe on a mirrored window could be taken for a pinch and not scroll at all.
