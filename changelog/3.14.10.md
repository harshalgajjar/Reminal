# 3.14.10

Fixed
- Starting a new session on a machine now takes a working directory like
  ~/project or just project, as the field suggests. Before, only a full
  path from the root was accepted.
- On a Mac, upgrading through the machine's background service now
  restarts every open session too. Before, the service could restart
  itself first and leave the sessions on the old version until they were
  restarted by hand.
