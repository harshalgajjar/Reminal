# 3.12.1

Fixed
- The swipe-up session list now shows which sessions need you without opening
  anything. It used to refresh only while the Machines screen was open, so the
  state pills in the quick switcher could be stale until you opened a session —
  now the list keeps itself current on its own, and refreshes the moment you
  swipe it up.
- On Linux Wayland machines, "View full desktop" now actually opens the
  screen. It was being blocked by the very note that explains Wayland's limits
  ("per-window control isn't supported here") — even though the full-desktop
  view works perfectly well on Wayland. The button now opens your desktop, and
  that note stays as a heads-up rather than standing in the way.
