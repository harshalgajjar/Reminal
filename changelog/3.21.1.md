# 3.21.1

Improved
- Your agent answers out loud without asking you first. `reminal integrate` now allows reminal's speak tool, and only that tool, in Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Qwen Code. Run `reminal integrate` again to get this.
- Qwen Code can answer voice out loud: it now loads reminal's tools when it starts instead of hiding them until it searches for them.
- Voice works with Gemini CLI, Cursor CLI and pi as soon as they start, once set up with `reminal integrate`: they now tell reminal when they are ready for a message. Run `reminal integrate` again to get this.

Fixed
- On a device with no voice of its own, voice no longer goes quiet without a word. With Auto it switches to the natural voice; if you picked the device's voice, it says the device has none. Your agent is told a line was not heard instead of being told it was spoken.
- When your agent cuts in while you talk at length, stopping to listen now works. Its "Sorry," plays at once, you get a moment to finish your word, and what you said after it cut in is no longer sent to it, so you hear what it wanted to say instead of an answer to your last few words.
- A goose session asking to allow a tool now shows as needing you, and goose not signed in to its provider shows as signed out.
