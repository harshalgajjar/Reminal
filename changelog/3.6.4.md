# 3.6.4

- The Host panel checks for a newer release by itself when you open it, and
  then shows one of two things: an upgrade to press, or "up to date". There is
  no button to check first.
- Everything about which reminal a machine runs now sits together at the
  bottom of the panel — version, state, "What's new", and the upgrade when
  there is one — under the machine's stats and the actions you open the panel
  for. The controls are smaller, and each version number appears once.
- A failed upgrade offers "Retry upgrade", with what went wrong written
  underneath, instead of putting the error text on the button.
