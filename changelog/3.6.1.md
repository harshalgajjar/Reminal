# 3.6.1

- "What's new" now opens on the version you are running, not only the ones you
  are missing. Your release is listed and marked "you are here", so reading
  what you got after an upgrade no longer means a trip to GitHub.

Fixes
- The panel was empty for anyone on the latest version. It listed only releases
  newer than yours, so being up to date and being unable to reach the notes
  looked exactly the same.
- The "releases behind" count no longer includes the version you are already
  on, and the upgrade button stays hidden when there is nothing newer to move
  to.
