# 3.13.12

Improved
- Installing reminal now walks you through setting it up, instead of printing
  commands to remember for later. It offers to grant the macOS permissions it
  needs, register reminal with the coding agents on your machine, and on a
  laptop keep serving with the lid shut and stop the Mac locking while you are
  away.
- Each step says what it gets you and what it costs, so you can decide. Skip
  any of them and they are listed at the end, ready to run whenever you want.
