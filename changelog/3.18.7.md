# 3.18.7

Added
- `reminal integrate` now sets up GitHub Copilot CLI: reminal's tools, plus whether Copilot is working, waiting for your approval or done. A Copilot that isn't signed in shows as logged out, and its rate limits show as limited.
- `reminal integrate` now sets up Kilo Code CLI the same way it sets up OpenCode. Your own Kilo settings file is left untouched.

Improved
- reminal now notices when Codex leaves commands running in the background after a turn: `reminal list` shows "done · 1 background terminal", and `reminal sleep` names them.
- `reminal info --json`, and the session list agents read through reminal, now include subagents and the other background work `reminal list` shows, not only shells.

Fixed
- A Kilo session waiting for your approval no longer shows as done.
- An OpenCode or Kilo session could be left showing as working after its turn had ended. Its updates now arrive one at a time, newest last, and reminal brings its OpenCode and Kilo plugins up to date by itself after an upgrade.
