# 3.22.7

Improved
- The reminal button in your browser now has two headings: `Sessions using this browser` and `Recent activity`. Before, the list of what agents did ran on from the list of sessions with nothing between them.
- The list of sessions shows only sessions that are running, or that still have their tab group open in this browser. A session that has ended and has no tabs left is no longer listed, and nothing is kept for it.
- When a session ends, its tab group now stays open, with its pages as the agent left them. The button shows that session as ended, with `Close its tabs`, which closes the tabs in that group and no others. Before, the group was undone and its tabs were left loose among yours.
- The button's panel draws its switches first, without waiting for the lists under them.
- While reminal is not connected, the panel lists the sessions that have a tab group and removes nothing.
- When the browser was connected before and is not now, reminal also says to press the extension's reload button, which is what helps after its files were put back.

Added
- A history page: `View all history` in the button's panel opens it in a tab. It lists everything kept, newest first, with a search box and filters by session, by kind of action and by time.
- reminal now keeps the pages an agent opened, as well as the scripts it ran in pages, the files it gave to pages, and the cookies and site storage it read or changed. For a page it keeps the time, the session, the site and the page's address. It does not keep clicks, typing, page titles or what a page says.
- This is kept in this browser only, for 30 days, and at most the newest 5,000 entries. Before, it was kept until the browser closed.
- You can clear it, as you clear a browser's history: `Clear history` on the history page removes the last hour, the last day, the last 7 days or all of it, after asking once, and `Remove` on a row removes that one entry.
- Each time the button's panel opens it writes one line to its own console saying how long it took to show. Nothing is sent anywhere. To read it, right-click the panel, choose Inspect, and open Console.
