# 3.18.10

Fixed
- A viewer that came back before its session did, after the relay restarted, said "session not found" and stopped. It now waits for the session for up to two minutes and is live again when the session returns. An unknown session or a wrong PIN still ends at once.
- After a restart, part of a session's history could come back twice: in a session started shortly before, or one resized many times just before the restart. It comes back once.
- A multi-line paste into a program right after joining was sent line by line, each line submitting. A join now leaves the program's settings as it had them, including bracketed paste, application keys and the mouse.
- A viewer reporting an impossibly large screen no longer sizes the session to it: sizes are capped at 1000 columns by 500 rows.
