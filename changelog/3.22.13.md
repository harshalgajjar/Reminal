# 3.22.13

Improved
- When a session ends, reminal now keeps a short note of why: its shell exited, it was asked to quit, it was put to sleep, or it was told to stop. If reminal itself runs into a fault, what it printed is kept too. These notes stay on your computer, in the `restore` folder inside `.reminal` in your home folder, and make it possible to say afterwards why a session went away.
