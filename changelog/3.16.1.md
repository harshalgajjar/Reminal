# 3.16.1

Fixed
- What you type while a session is still opening now arrives in that session. It is no longer lost, and it never goes to the session you are switching away from.
- A machine no longer sometimes says its identity changed right after it starts.
- Links from a terminal or a host only open when they are web pages.
- The top bar stays on one row on every screen. When space is tight the buttons show just their icons, and their names come back when there is room.
- An agent that is simply idle no longer shows "needs you" because of words in its reply.

Improved
- Actions you take as a machine's owner can't be replayed after reminal restarts.
