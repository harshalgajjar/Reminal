# 3.13.0

Improved
- Web apps you share with `reminal expose` now work even when they expect to
  live at the root of their own site. Each forwarded port gets its own address
  (like `port-ABCD1234.reminal.app`) instead of a shared `/p/…` path, so admin
  panels and dashboards that load assets from `/`, register a service worker, or
  enforce same-origin — webmin, UniFi and the like — come up and let you log in,
  the same way they do on a Cloudflare quick tunnel. Older `/p/…` links keep
  working.
