# 3.13.25

Improved
- A session whose AI agent has been signed out now says "logged out", in
  `reminal list` and on your Machines page. It used to look finished, so
  you'd leave it alone while it quietly did nothing. Works with Claude Code,
  Codex, Gemini CLI, Qwen Code, Amp and the Antigravity CLI.
- Long messages sent into an AI agent's session now arrive in one piece,
  like a paste, instead of trickling down the screen one letter per line.
- Codex and Cursor agents now light up "working" and "needs you" on your
  sessions like the other agents do. Before, some of those updates never
  showed up.

Fixed
- `reminal copy` and `reminal paste` now work on your own self-hosted relay
  too, instead of stopping with "bad handshake".
- Session names shown in `reminal machines` can no longer mess up your
  terminal or disguise what they really say.
