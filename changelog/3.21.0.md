# 3.21.0

Added
- Talk to your agent and hear it answer. Tap the mic in a session, speak, and what you say is typed in for the agent; it answers out loud.
- Hearing your voice and making the agent's voice both happen on your own device. Only the text of what you said leaves it.
- Talk at length: what you have said so far reaches the agent every few seconds, so it can follow along, and it can cut in if you asked it to.
- Talk over it to stop it. Hold while you think, so a pause does not end your turn. Mute to talk to someone else in the room.
- Swipe up on the mic to start, stop or pause listening without opening the panel.
- A natural voice on computers, and your device's own voice on phones, or pick either in the settings.

Safety
- Words you say are typed in only when an agent is waiting for a message. They are not typed into a question reminal sees the agent asking on screen, into a plain terminal, or at a password prompt; you are told when that happens.
- Every part of the voice feature is checked against the exact version this reminal expects before it is used.

Good to know
- The first time you start voice it downloads its models once, about 100 MB. After that it starts in seconds.
- Your agent needs reminal's speak tool to answer out loud. Agents set up with `reminal integrate` have it.
- Tested with Claude Code. With other agents, voice may not recognise every question they ask on screen.
- Voice needs a recent desktop or Android browser. Tested on Chrome; Safari, iPhone, Firefox and sessions on Windows are not tested yet.
- A session on a machine with an older reminal says that voice needs a newer reminal there.
