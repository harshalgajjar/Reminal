# 3.10.2

Fixed
- A session that finished and went quiet no longer shows up as **needs you**.
  reminal now tells apart an agent that's genuinely waiting on you — a question
  or a permission to approve — from one that simply wrapped up and is sitting
  idle at the prompt. Only the first lights up amber, so a fleet of finished
  agents stays calm instead of all clamouring for attention after a minute.
- `reminal expose` no longer bounces visitors to their own machine. If the site
  you're sharing issued a redirect (adding a trailing slash, forcing HTTPS, a
  canonical host), the visitor's browser could get sent to `127.0.0.1` — i.e.
  *their* computer, where nothing's running. Those redirects now stay on the
  public link.
- Large images, GIFs, and downloads served through `reminal expose` come through
  in full. Anything bigger than about 700 KB used to be silently cut off
  partway; responses now stream in their entirety.
