# 3.6.5

Fixes
- The Host panel could say "up to date" beside a "What's new" that listed a
  newer version. The status and the notes were answered from two different
  places with two different ages. They now come from one release list on the
  host, so what the panel offers is always the newest release it can show
  notes for — and "up to date" is only said once a fresh answer has arrived.
- Opening the panel makes one request, not two, and a machine that keeps the
  panel open cannot exceed GitHub's request limit.
