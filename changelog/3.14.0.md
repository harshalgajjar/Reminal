# 3.14.0

Added
- Alerts from your machines. Tap the bell next to the search box in your
  Machines panel and every machine you own can notify you, even with
  reminal closed: when its CPU stays too high or too low for a while, when
  its battery drops below a level or below an amount of time left, or when
  its charger is plugged in or out.
- One set of rules for all your machines, or custom rules for any one of
  them. Every battery alert tells you both the charge and the time left.
- Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on your phone or your
  desktop. On iPhone and iPad, add reminal to your Home Screen first.
- Private by design: each alert is locked to your own device before it
  leaves your machine, so nothing in between can read it.
