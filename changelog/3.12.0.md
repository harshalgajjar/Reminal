# 3.12.0

Improved
- `reminal expose` now shares far more of what runs on your machine. It reaches
  local sites that use https — router, NAS and server admin panels like UniFi,
  Proxmox and webmin — instead of failing to connect to them. And it now carries
  live connections, so apps that update in real time (Node-RED, Jupyter
  notebooks, dev servers with live reload, chat and dashboards) work through the
  shared link exactly as they do on your own screen, instead of loading to a
  blank page. Pages that stream their updates — live logs, progress, activity
  feeds — now tick along as they happen instead of sitting still and arriving
  all at once.

Fixed
- Pages shared with `reminal expose` no longer arrive as a screenful of garbled
  characters. Some servers hand their pages over compressed; those now come
  through readable, the way you'd see them locally.
