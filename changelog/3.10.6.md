- A session that's asking you something now shows **needs you**, not "done".
  When an agent ends its turn on a question — a multiple-choice prompt, a
  yes/no, an approval — it reports the turn as finished, so the pill used to
  read "done" even though it was waiting on your answer. reminal now notices the
  question still on screen and marks it **needs you** so it stands out.
