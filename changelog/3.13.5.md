# 3.13.5

Improved
- The session switcher now refreshes every second for the sessions you're
  choosing between, and every five seconds for the rest of your machines — so
  which session needs you is effectively live. It does this without draining
  your phone or hammering the relay: each of your machines is asked over a
  connection that stays open, instead of reconnecting and re-authenticating on
  every check. It pauses entirely while the tab is in the background.
