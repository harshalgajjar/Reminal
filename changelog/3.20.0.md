# 3.20.0

- Every session now runs on a new terminal engine, so what you see on your phone, tablet or second screen follows the real terminal more faithfully.
- Resizing a window, rotating your phone or joining from a device with a different screen size keeps much more of your history: about half as many lines go missing as before.
- Sessions use about half the memory they did.
- A burst of heavy output, like a long build log, is taken in many times faster.

Fixed
- After an update or restart, a session with a very long history could come back with none of it on a slow or busy machine. It now comes back whole.
