Fixed
- A session no longer keeps saying "needs you" after you have already answered
  it. When you reply to a question an agent asked — a multiple-choice prompt, a
  permission — it carries straight on working without announcing it, so the
  "needs you" mark could stick for the rest of a long turn over a session that
  was busy. reminal now notices the session is still producing output and marks
  it working again, while a session that really is waiting keeps its mark for as
  long as it takes you to get to it.
