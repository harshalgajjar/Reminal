# 3.8.1

Fixes
- The viewer could get stuck on "Reconnecting…" and never come back — you'd have
  to reload the page to recover. It usually happened right after your phone
  switched networks (Wi-Fi to 5G, or between Wi-Fi points), which can leave the
  connection silently frozen with no error for either side to notice. The viewer
  now spots a connection that stops responding and retries on its own, so it
  comes back by itself instead of hanging.
