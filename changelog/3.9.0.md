# 3.9.0

New
- Close a window on the computer itself, right from its mirror. On a window
  you've opened, swipe up from its ✕ button and choose "Close on the machine":
  the real window closes on the host — with the app still free to ask about
  unsaved changes — instead of just disappearing from your view here. A plain
  tap on ✕ still only stops viewing it, so hiding and truly closing can't be
  mixed up. Works on macOS, Linux, and Windows.
