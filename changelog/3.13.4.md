# 3.13.4

Fixed
- Session state pills no longer get stuck. A session that finished a command
  and returned to its prompt could keep showing "working" (or "done" / "needs
  you") indefinitely — the "back to idle" change was silently dropped on the way
  to your screen, and once a live state was shown nothing could ever clear it.
  Idle is now reported explicitly, and whichever update is newest always wins,
  so every pill tracks reality and corrects itself within seconds.
