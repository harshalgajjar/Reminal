# 3.22.8

Added
- For browsers an administrator manages: the browser's policy can now set the starting value of three switches in the reminal button: scripts in pages, files given to pages, and cookies and site storage.
- Your own switch always wins. Once you set a switch in the button, on or off, the policy no longer changes it.
- With no policy, as on your own computer, nothing changes: all three stay off until you switch them on.
- The policy cannot change the main switch, agents' use of this browser. That one is yours alone.
