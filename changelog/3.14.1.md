# 3.14.1

Improved
- Alerts now arrive within seconds. Unplug your laptop and your phone
  knows almost before you have put the cable down.
- Plug it back in and you get that alert too, every time. A late
  "unplugged" can no longer hide the "connected" that came after it.
- Battery and CPU alerts fire the moment your rule is met, not up to a
  minute later.
- If an alert does reach you late, for example because your phone was
  offline, it tells you the time it actually happened.
