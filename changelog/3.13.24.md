# 3.13.24

Fixed
- Upgrading a Windows computer from your phone or browser no longer says
  "Upgrade failed" when it actually worked. If you were sharing a local port
  with `reminal expose`, the upgrade went through but reported a failure, and
  the session you were watching stayed on the old version. It now finishes
  properly and ends on "Up to date".
- The Upgrade button in What's new now shows "Up to date" when it's done,
  like the one in the Host menu, instead of spinning on "Restarting" forever.
