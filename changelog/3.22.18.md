# 3.22.18

Improved
- Typing in a session is much more responsive when your device can reach the computer directly, for example on the same Wi-Fi: what you type and what comes back now also travel on that direct connection, and whichever arrives first is used. The usual route stays in use beside it, so nothing is lost if the direct one drops.
- Kiro CLI 3.6 changed its wordings: reminal again notices when it needs you to sign in, when it is being rate-limited, and when a tool is waiting for your approval.
